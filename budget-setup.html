SETUP NOTES

Build a cleaner gaming desk on a budget

A better setup doesn't have to mean buying a new desk, monitor and a whole drawer of accessories.

Start with the layout

Put the things you use most within easy reach. Keep the keyboard and mouse area clear, and place the monitor so you can look at it without constantly bending your neck.

Sort cables before buying accessories

Unplug devices safely, group cables by destination and use reusable ties if needed. Measure cable routes before buying longer cables or clips so you don't spend money on things that don't fit.

Prioritise what you notice

If your chair or desk height is uncomfortable, fix that before buying decorative lighting. If your mouse keeps slipping, a suitable mouse pad may be more useful than another RGB accessory.

Keep power and airflow in mind

Don't overload power strips or block laptop vents. Leave space around equipment that gets warm and avoid routing cables where chair wheels can damage them.

Make upgrades one at a time

Use the setup for a week and note what annoys you most. Fix the biggest problem first, then reassess. This keeps upgrades practical and helps avoid buying things that look good but do little.

These are general setup tips. Adapt them to your room, equipment and budget.
