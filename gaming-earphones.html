What to look for in budget gaming earphones

A low-cost pair can be great for gaming, but the right choice depends on what you play and which device you use.

1. Comfort comes first

If you play for an hour or more at a time, fit matters. Look for multiple ear-tip sizes and a shape that stays comfortable. A popular model is not automatically the right fit for every ear.

2. Think about the microphone

For team games and calls, check whether the inline microphone picks up your voice clearly. If possible, read reviews that discuss voice quality in a normal room rather than only music playback.

3. Wired or wireless?

Wired earphones are usually straightforward and don't need charging. Wireless models are convenient, but latency, battery life and device compatibility deserve a closer look if you play competitive games.

4. Check your connection

Some phones do not have a 3.5 mm headphone jack. A USB-C earphone or a compatible USB-C DAC may be needed. Check your device's support before ordering, especially if the product listing is unclear.

5. Read the boring details

Check warranty coverage, return conditions, cable durability and replacement availability. These details can matter more than a long list of marketing terms.

Bottom line: decide your budget, confirm compatibility, then compare comfort, mic performance and reliable user feedback. Don't buy based on a specification alone.

This is a general buying guide, not a hands-on review of a specific product. Product prices and availability change.
