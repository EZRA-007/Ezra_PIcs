TECH BASICS

Before buying a USB-C cable

USB-C describes the connector shape. It doesn't automatically tell you how fast a cable charges or transfers data.

Check charging support

Look for the cable's supported power rating and make sure it matches your charger and device. Fast charging also depends on the phone, charger and supported charging standard working together.

Don't ignore data speed

Some cables are designed mainly for charging and offer only basic data transfer. If you regularly move large files or connect accessories, check the listed USB data standard rather than assuming every USB-C cable is the same.

Choose a useful length

A short cable can be tidy on a desk, while a longer one may be more practical beside a bed. Very long or poorly made cables can be inconvenient, so choose a length that suits where you use it.

Look for clear product information

Check the manufacturer's specifications, warranty and seller details. Be cautious when a listing makes big performance claims but doesn't clearly state supported power or data rates.

One last check

Use a reputable charger and cable, inspect them for damage and stop using a cable that becomes unusually hot or has exposed wiring.

Always verify compatibility and specifications for your particular device before purchasing.
